## Kinet Pricing Model: **Completely Free**

### Principles
1. **100% free forever** — no subscription, no paywall, no feature gates
2. **No Supporter tier** — no cloud sync, no advanced AI, no tiers at all
3. **No Stripe, no billing, no payments** — zero payment infrastructure
4. **Sustainable via** — GitHub Sponsors (optional), Patreon (optional), or purely passion project
