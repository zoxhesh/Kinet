## 2. The Problem

### Paywalls That Block You Before You Start
Most fitness apps gate core features behind subscriptions. Arise forces you to pay or invite three friends before you can even log a single workout. You can't try before you commit.

### No Nutrition in Your Fitness RPG
A fitness RPG that ignores nutrition is only half the game. You track workouts but not what fuels them — no calories, no macros, no AI insights connecting the two.

### Progress Loss When Life Happens
Get sick, get injured, need a rest day? Most apps don't let you pause a workout. You lose progress or fake your way through.

## 6. FAQ

**Can I import from Strong/Hevy/Arise?**
Yes — CSV import for workouts and body stats. JSON export/import for full backup.

**Is it really free forever?**
Yes. All features are free forever. No subscription, no feature gates, no paywall.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. All features work offline. Your data stays on your device.

**What about cloud sync?**
Local-first design. Manual CSV/JSON export/import for backup. No cloud sync (yet).

**What about my data?**
You own it. Export anytime as CSV or JSON. Delete account anytime — data wiped in 24 hours.

**How does AI analysis work?**
Post-workout, Kinet analyzes your sets, volume, RPE trends, and nutrition (if logged) to give a 30-second summary: volume trend, fatigue signals, protein adequacy, next-week suggestions. Runs locally on device.

**Can I track custom exercises?**
Yes. Create your own with name, muscle group, equipment, difficulty, instructions, and GIF/video.

**What happens if I get injured mid-workout?**
Tap Pause. Resume tomorrow or discard. No progress lost. Safety first.
