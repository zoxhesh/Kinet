## Step 4: Logo Brief

**Name**: Kinet  
**Tagline**: *Forge your motion.*

### Concept
"Kinet" from **kinetic** — energy in motion. The logo captures the moment of potential becoming kinetic energy: a single line accelerating into motion, like a rep beginning.

### Mark Type
**Symbol + Wordmark** — symbol works alone at 16px (favicon), combines with wordmark for app icon and web.

### Symbol Concept
A single continuous line that starts static (dot) and accelerates into a dynamic curve, suggesting the transition from potential to kinetic energy. The curve suggests both a rep trajectory and an upward growth chart.

- **At 16px**: Simplified to a single curved stroke with acceleration dots
- **At 1024px**: Full symbol with wordmark "Kinet" in custom geometric sans

### Color Application
- **Symbol**: Brand Primary (`#06B6D4` light / `#22D3EE` dark)
- **Wordmark**: Text Primary (dark) / Text Inverse (light)
- **Background**: App icon — Brand Muted (`#CFFAFE` light / `#164E63` dark) with Symbol in Brand Primary

### Deliverables
| Asset | Specs |
|-------|-------|
| Logo SVG | Scalable, no transparency issues |
| App Icon iOS | 1024×1024, no transparency, rounded corners applied by OS |
| App Icon Android | 512×512, adaptive icon (foreground + background) |
| Favicon Set | 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256px |
| Social Image | 1200×630, OG card |

### Must Not Resemble
- Arise logo (no upward arrow, no "level up" staircase, no orange)
- No shared letterform tricks (Arise uses custom A; Kinet uses geometric K)

## Step 5: Voice

**Three Words**: **Direct, Encouraging, Smart**

| Word | Means | Does NOT Mean |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Direct** | Clear, concise, no fluff | Not blunt or robotic |
| **Encouraging** | Celebrates progress, human tone | Not cheerleading or fake |
| **Smart** | Respects user's intelligence, explains why | Not condescending or academic |
